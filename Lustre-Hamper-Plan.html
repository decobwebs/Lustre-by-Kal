Five kitchen and home appliances

Three ways to use them

Swaps

A company swaps one item for another, say the steamer instead of the iron. It's easy to offer, but the price stays the same, so only swap things that cost about the same.

A fourth hamper

A kitchen and home hamper built around the air fryer, blender and coffee maker. It gives companies a different kind of gift, but it's one more hamper to price, photograph and keep in stock.

Add‑ons

Our suggestion

Any hamper plus an appliance, for an extra price. For example, 40 Grace hampers, with an air fryer added to the 5 for managers. Your three prices stay simple, and companies can choose to spend more.

Fix first

Things to fix before anything goes live

The Christmas card has another company's contact details

The back of the card shows your logo, but the phone numbers, email, website and address next to it belong to Reliant Anchor Logistics. The design file itself is named “reliant anchor”, so the designer probably reused an earlier job. If these cards are printed, your clients will contact the wrong company.

Fix: ask the designer to put Lustre's own details on the card, unless you run both businesses and want it this way.

Your cheaper hampers include things the top one doesn't

A manager who gets Radiant (₦360,000) might see a supplier's Bloom box (₦160,000) with a kettle and a humidifier, two things Radiant doesn't have. They'll ask why theirs cost ₦200,000 more.
